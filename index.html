Cybersecurity

From a Wikipedia-inspired educational article

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks and digital information from unauthorized access, misuse and online threats.

Overview

Digital security is important for individuals, businesses and organizations. Safe online habits can help reduce risks when using the internet.

Common threats

Common threats include malicious software, suspicious links, stolen passwords and attempts to access accounts without permission.

Password security

Strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication help protect online accounts. Passwords should never be shared with strangers.

Phishing

Phishing is a technique in which a message or website tries to trick someone into sharing private information. Check links carefully before opening them.
